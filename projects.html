PhD research

Work from the RISE Lab at ASU, published or under review.

Dual quaternion collision recovery

A closed-form hybrid reset map on the dual quaternion manifold that keeps normal–tangential impulse coupling in a single algebraic operation. Proven hybrid Lyapunov stability, 25% fewer FLOPs than the matrix formulation.

  • Hybrid systems
  • SE(3)
  • MuJoCo
  • L-CSS 2026

Switch-time-aware hybrid MPC

Lifting the switching instant into the decision vector so a real-time SQP solver can optimise impact timing. KKT conditions consistent with the Hybrid Minimum Principle, and a 116× speedup over complementarity formulations.

  • Optimal control
  • MPC
  • Hybrid Minimum Principle
  • CDC 2026

Coursework

Graduate coursework at Arizona State University, separate from my research. Both built on a Dobot Magician 4-DOF arm with an overhead camera.

Vision-based maze solving

A 4-DOF arm reads a printed maze from an overhead camera and traces the solution: affine hand–eye calibration to 1.34 mm, perspective rectification, occupancy grid, and BFS path planning.

  • OpenCV
  • Calibration
  • Path planning
  • RAS 545

LLM-driven pick and place

Plain-English commands drive the arm, with Gemini function calling acting as the controller — resolving spatial references like “on top of” into calibrated metric offsets.

  • LLM tool use
  • Gemini
  • HSV segmentation
  • RAS 545
